Scientific vision

Understanding the dynamic organization of cognitive processes through multimodal neuroimaging and computational modeling of brain activity.

Research axes

  • Multimodal integration (MEG / EEG / fMRI / SEEG)
  • Temporal dynamics of brain activity
  • Neural representations (RSA)
  • Machine learning in cognitive neuroscience

Platform perspective

Strong focus on bridging methodological development and experimental neuroscience, ensuring reproducibility, data quality and user support in shared infrastructures.
