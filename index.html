Research Profile

I am a Senior Research Engineer specializing in cognitive neuroscience, multimodal neuroimaging, and scientific platform support. My work focuses on integrating MEG, EEG, SEEG, and functional MRI data to investigate the neural mechanisms underlying perception, cognition, and behavior.

I provide methodological expertise across the entire experimental workflow, including study design, data acquisition, signal and imaging processing, advanced statistical analysis, machine learning approaches, and reproducible research practices.

My current activities also include contributing to the development of scientific platforms, providing user support and training, managing research data, and implementing Open Science and FAIR data principles.

Current Position

Research Engineer in the Institute of Convergence "Language, Communication and the Brain" (ILCB, LPL, Aix-Marseille University), providing methodological and technical expertise for multidisciplinary research projects involving MEG, EEG, SEEG, and functional MRI.

Activities include experimental design support, multimodal data analysis, development of Python-based analysis pipelines, supervision and training of researchers and students, and participation in national and international collaborative projects.
