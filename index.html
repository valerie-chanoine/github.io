Research Profile

I am a Senior Research Engineer specializing in cognitive neuroscience, multimodal neuroimaging, and scientific platform support. My work focuses on integrating MEG, EEG, SEEG, and functional MRI data to investigate the neural mechanisms underlying perception, cognition, and behavior.

I provide methodological expertise across the entire experimental workflow, including study design, data acquisition, signal processing, advanced statistical analysis, machine learning approaches, and reproducible research practices.

My current activities also include scientific platform development, user support and training, research data management, and the implementation of open science and FAIR data principles.
